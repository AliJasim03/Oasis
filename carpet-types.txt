Handmade Qum silk carpets are among the finest and most luxurious Persian rugs ever crafted. Woven in the historic city of Qum in Iran, these masterpieces are made entirely from premium natural silk, giving them an unmatched softness, delicate sheen, and vibrant color depth. Each carpet is meticulously knotted by skilled artisans, often featuring intricate floral motifs, medallions, and traditional Persian patterns that can take months—or even years—to complete. Because of their exceptional knot density, precision, and artistic detail, Qum silk carpets are highly sought after by collectors worldwide. They are not only elegant decorative pieces but also valuable investments that embody centuries of Persian craftsmanship and cultural heritage.

Nain handmade carpets are renowned for their refined elegance, exceptional craftsmanship, and timeless Persian designs. Originating from the city of Nain in central Iran, these carpets are traditionally woven with high-quality wool and often decorated with silk highlights that bring out the beauty of their intricate patterns. Nain rugs are famous for their soft palettes—typically ivory, beige, and light blues—along with floral motifs and medallion designs. Each carpet is carefully woven with a high knot density that reflects generations of skilled craftsmanship.

Nain carpets come in three main grades based on knot density:
•⁠  ⁠Nohla (9-La) – A high-quality type made with nine layers of thread. Durable, elegant, and more affordable than the finer grades.
•⁠  ⁠Shishla (6-La) – A finer and more detailed grade woven with six layers. It has higher knot density, sharper patterns, and more silk highlights.
•⁠  ⁠Chahla / Sharla (4-La) – The rarest and finest grade. Woven with four layers, allowing extremely detailed designs, very high knot count, and premium silk accents. These are collector-level carpets.

وتأتي سجاد نائين بثلاث درجات رئيسية حسب كثافة العقد وجودة الخيوط:

Isfahan handmade carpets are considered some of the most prestigious and artistically refined Persian rugs in the world. Originating from the historic city of Isfahan, these carpets are woven with exceptionally fine wool—often combined with luxurious silk—to create detailed and elegant designs. Isfahan rugs are known for their classical Persian motifs, including Shah Abbasi flowers, vines, medallions, and arabesque patterns, all arranged with perfect balance and symmetry. Their color palettes usually feature rich tones such as ivory, navy blue, burgundy, and deep red, giving each carpet a royal and timeless appearance.

Isfahan carpets are typically woven on a silk or cotton foundation with a very high knot density, allowing the weavers to produce extremely sharp and detailed designs. Master workshops such as Seirafian, Haghighi, and Davari are globally recognized for producing some of the finest Isfahan rugs ever made. Because of their superior craftsmanship, artistic beauty, and durability, Isfahan carpets are treasured by collectors and are often considered investment pieces of lasting cultural value.

Kashan handmade carpets are among the most iconic and historically significant Persian rugs, known for their rich tradition, durability, and elegant artistry. Woven in the city of Kashan—one of the oldest carpet-weaving centers in Iran—these rugs are crafted using high-quality wool and often highlighted with touches of silk. Kashan carpets are famous for their classic central medallion designs, floral motifs, and deep, luxurious colors such as ruby red, navy blue, and ivory. Their dense weaving and balanced patterns make them ideal for both traditional and modern interiors. Highly valued for their strength and timeless beauty, Kashan carpets remain a cornerstone of Persian carpet craftsmanship.

Tabriz carpets are celebrated for their exceptional craftsmanship, artistic range, and rich cultural heritage. Afghani handmade carpets are known for their bold tribal designs, deep natural colors, and strong wool construction. Traditionally woven by Afghan tribes such as the Turkmen and Baluchi, these rugs use hand-spun wool dyed with natural pigments, giving them a warm and earthy character. Common patterns include the famous “Elephant Foot” (Gul) motifs, geometric shapes, and repeating medallions. The colors—typically deep red, burgundy, black, and dark blue—create a powerful and traditional aesthetic. Durable and rich in cultural identity, Afghani carpets are admired for their authenticity, craftsmanship, and lasting beauty.

Kazak carpets, originally woven in the Caucasus region, are celebrated for their bold geometric patterns, vibrant colors, and rustic charm. Modern Kazak rugs—often woven in Afghanistan using traditional Caucasian designs—are made with high-quality hand-spun wool and natural dyes. Their patterns include large medallions, tribal symbols, crosses, diamonds, and angular motifs that give the carpet a strong visual identity. The colors—bright reds, blues, greens, and creams—make Kazak rugs stand out as statement pieces in any home. Durable, culturally rich, and full of personality, Kazak carpets are ideal for customers seeking both heritage and vibrant design.
